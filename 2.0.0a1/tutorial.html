Tutorial

A guide to basic usage of xtalmet is shown in README.md. We also provide a more detailed tutorial notebook here.

Distances and evaluation

Distance matrices remain dense float64 arrays. Failed embeddings produce NaN rows and columns, including the diagonal; nonfinite pair distances are also NaN. Failed generated embeddings receive zero uniqueness and novelty scores, while invalid references are excluded. Novelty requires at least one valid reference; continuous uniqueness requires at least two generated structures. See distance_matrix() and evaluate() for the calculation and failure rules.

Set multiprocessing and n_processes explicitly for large inputs. Constructor settings control reference preparation; evaluate() settings control generated embeddings and matrices. PDD’s args_dist["n_jobs"] overrides the matrix worker count. A 10,000 by 30,000 matrix alone uses about 2.24 GiB; component matrices, temporary arrays and workers require additional memory.

References and caches

Use save_reference_embeddings() to save custom references with their distance and embedding settings. Legacy plain embedding files must be regenerated from structures. Non-default MP20 embedding settings issue a warning and recompute references from the pinned training structures during initialization.

Passing dir_intermediate to evaluate() stores validated records under cache/format-1/<identity>/. Reuse requires matching ordered inputs, relevant settings, references, model/phase data and software. Old unversioned files are preserved and ignored; invalid records are recomputed. The notebook demonstrates reference-file round trips and describes cache reuse in more detail.
